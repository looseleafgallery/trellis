### Changed

- **`review` shows one decision, framed as one.** The session exists to
  collect a decision, so the finding, its remedy and the keys are presented
  together rather than as a run of similar-looking lines.
- **Progress is a block rule rather than a fraction.** The number is still
  there — it is the one you quote when you stop halfway — but you no longer
  have to read it to see how far in you are. The running tally of what you
  have answered rides alongside it.
- **The option keys are amber and their labels dim**, so a row reads as a set
  of options rather than as a sentence. Amber still means exactly one thing:
  a person is owed a decision, which is what a key is.
