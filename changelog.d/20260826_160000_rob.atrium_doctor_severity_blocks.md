### Fixed

- **`doctor` sorts by urgency again inside each severity.** `Problem.rank`
  says the order is severity, then urgency, then node — but `doctor` re-sorted
  the assembled list by severity and *node*, which silently flattened urgency
  to alphabetical. Two dangling references and two gate errors interleaved by
  node name, when nothing else can be trusted until the dangling references
  are gone.
- **Severity glyphs no longer collide with readiness marks.** `warn` and
  `info` were `?` and `.`, which are also the marks for `awaiting` and
  `unagreed`/`draft`. They are now `▲` and `i`, and a test holds the two sets
  disjoint.

### Changed

- **`doctor` groups its findings into three severity blocks.** A flat list
  invited severity and urgency to be read as one scale — position looked like
  importance, when position inside a severity only ever meant "fix this one
  first".
- **Finding codes are shown**, at the right edge and dim: greppable, and out
  of the way of the sentence that says what is wrong. They were previously
  used only to look up a remedy and never printed.
- **The header counts findings by severity** and says how many were
  acknowledged and not shown. `check_with_muted` already returned that number
  and `doctor` was discarding it.
