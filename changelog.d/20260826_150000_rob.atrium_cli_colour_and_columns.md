### Added

- **Colour in the human commands, carrying meaning rather than decoration.**
  Four 256-colour values: amber means a person is owed a decision and appears
  nowhere else, red means blocked or a defect, green means ready or verified,
  dim means settled or scaffolding. Colour is dropped entirely when stdout is
  not a tty, when `NO_COLOR` is set, or under `--json`, and every mark, code
  and word survives that — the plain output is the previous output plus better
  columns.
- **`--ascii`**, which restores the `|-` and `` `- `` branches. Trees are drawn
  with box-drawing characters by default, falling back to ASCII on their own
  when the terminal cannot encode them.
- **`trellis graph -f tree` now says what the slice waits on**, and prefixes the
  slice with what it is and how many nodes it holds.

### Changed

- **The tree's status column no longer moves between slices.** It was aligned
  to the widest readiness word present, so the column jumped every time the
  slice changed. It is now a constant width, which is the point of a column.
